🔤 SWAPLE

Arcade Word Puzzle Mechanics

  • Place Letters: Click empty slots to place incoming letters from your bag.
  • Swap Tiles: Click a filled tile and another tile to swap their positions.
  • Heavy Tiles (Q, W, X, Z): Cost 2 swaps instead of 1 to move!
  • Bomb Tiles (💣): Defuse them by placing the correct letter within 4 turns, or they explode (-10s penalty).
  • Freeze Power: Sacrifice 1 swap and 10s to freeze the clock for 8 seconds.
